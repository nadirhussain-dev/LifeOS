- Use Daykeep to **evade a block or a suspension**, including by creating another

### 6.2 Reporting and blocking

In any expense group you can:

- **Report** the group, or a person in it, from the flag icon. Reports go to us
  and carry the specific item you complained about so it can be reviewed.
- **Block** another person. A block stops them adding you to a group, inviting
  you, or redeeming an invitation they sent you before you blocked them. It
  works in both directions.

A block does **not** remove either of you from a group you already share,
because that would change the group's ledger for everybody else. Leave the group
if you want out of it.

You can review and undo your blocks in **Settings → Blocked accounts**.

### 6.3 What we do about reports

We review reports and may, depending on what we find: leave the content alone,
remove it, restrict what an account can do, block an account, or delete it.
Serious cases — in particular anything involving a child, or a credible threat
to somebody's safety — may be reported to law enforcement.

We aim to act on reports promptly. We are one person, not a 24-hour moderation
desk, and we would rather say so here than imply a response time we cannot meet.
**If somebody is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services
first.** Reporting something in an app is not an emergency service.

### 6.4 If we act against your account

We will tell you in the app, with the reason, unless telling you would put
somebody at risk or we are legally prevented from doing so. You can appeal by
email to the address at the top of this document, and we will look at it.

A blocked account cannot read its own synced data, so if you need a copy of it
for a data-access request, say so in your appeal and we will produce it by hand.

## 7. Operator access to your data

Read this and section "What Daykeep staff can access" in the

This is a deliberate product decision so that abuse can be investigated. The app
tells you before you choose a PIN. If that is not acceptable to you, do not
create a private space on an account.
