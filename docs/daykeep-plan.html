Daykeep Rename · listing · braid · module bar Plan for review

Stop competing on features. Own one idea.

Four pieces of work in dependency order. The first three are days and produce a launch package. The fourth is the real work, and it is sequenced rather than attempted all at once.

The standard

What “best in market” has to mean

The competitor is good, has a head start on polish, and owns a strong visual idea. Matching its feature list produces parity — and parity loses to the incumbent every time.

1

Win a market they are not contesting

Urdu, Hindi and Arabic with real RTL is work Western indie developers don't do — and you have already done it. #1 for “habit tracker” in Pakistan is reachable. #5 globally is not, and is worth less.

2

Be famous for one thing

Fourteen modules is a positioning problem, not an asset. Three streaks, one year, something real in the post is the hook. Everything else is depth you reveal later.

3

Compete on trust

Encrypted at rest, a private vault, offline-first. A cloud-first competitor would have to rebuild to match it — and you are currently not claiming any of it.

Explicitly not doing: an AI coach (they have one; it is table stakes), social feeds, or a mortality calendar. The last is theirs, and copying it would read as exactly what it was.
And a standard worth saying out loud in the listing: the app does not manipulate. Streaks show missed days honestly instead of hiding them. Notifications name what is outstanding rather than manufacturing anxiety. No social comparison, no engagement bait. It is the right thing, and it is very hard for a competitor to adopt after the fact.
Work 1

The rename, and the one edit that must never happen

A global find-and-replace of lifeos → daykeep would permanently destroy every user's data

Read first

Twelve storage keys carry the old name. Four of them are load-bearing, and two are unrecoverable.

KeyWhat happens if it changes
daykeep.dbThe app opens an empty database
daykeep.db.keyThe existing database becomes permanently unreadable
daykeep.vault.wrapped / .alt / .saltThe private vault becomes permanently unrecoverable
daykeep.device-id · daykeep.install.idIdentity resets; 0048 demands a fresh OTP
daykeep.widget.today.v1 · .actions.v1Widget snapshot and queued taps lost
SoThese are not brand surfaces — nobody ever sees them. They stay exactly as they are, and a new test asserts all twelve verbatim, because this is precisely the kind of edit that looks like tidying up.

What actually changes

Identity
name, slug, and the lifeos → daykeep URL scheme, which moves every deep link with it.
Bundle id
com.daykeep.app → com.daykeep.app. Last chance — permanent after the first store publish.
Android widgets
DaykeepToday / DaykeepHabits. The name must match the plugin config and the task-handler map, or widgets silently stop rendering.
Copy
43 occurrences × 4 locales = 172 strings, plus ~25 source files. Includes the share card's “Tracked with Daykeep”.
Outside the repo
Brand assets, EAS project, Sentry project, Supabase redirect URL.
Work 2

The listing, led by the challenge

Your title is three characters too long

Needs a decision

Daykeep — Habits, Journal & Money is 33 characters. Google Play allows 30.

TitleCharsComment
Daykeep: Habits & Journal25Cleanest. Money is discoverable inside

Why“Habit tracker” is what people type; “life tracker” catches the broader intent. The brand does the remembering, the keywords do the finding.

The 80 characters that matter most

The short description appears in search results and above the fold. It is the highest-leverage text in the whole listing.

“Three daily habits. One year. A real gift in the post if you never miss.”

71 characters. The whole product in one line, unlike anything else in the category.

Description order

The promise → how it works → why it's different → then the module list → private by design → languages. The rules of the challenge are the marketing; state them plainly rather than describing features.

Screenshots — the first two decide the install

1. The braid mid-run. 2. Today's checklist with one item left — the daily reality. 3. The ladder with the prize visible. 4. The vault. 5. Money. 6. A shot in Urdu or Arabic, invisible to competitors and decisive locally.

Write all four listings properly, not machine-translated. The Urdu, Hindi and Arabic listings are close to uncontested. It is the cheapest ranking available to you, and the competitor is not going to do it.
Work 3

The braid

One strand per committed module, woven day by day. The picture and the mechanic are the same object — and nobody else has the three-module contract, so nobody else can have this image.

a strand held a module missed — the strand breaks a shield spliced the gap
What it encodes
A clean weave is a day everything held. A gap in one strand is that module missed. A shield shows as a splice — spliced, not broken. A fall is where the braid thins.
How it's drawn
react-native-svg only. The repo has no chart library and should not gain one. Phase-shifted sine paths, stroked in each module's tint, newest on the right.
Motion
Only today's segment animates — a slow draw-on. Everything else is static: motion everywhere is what makes an app feel cheap. Disabled under reduced-motion.
It replaces
day-chain.tsx and module-chains.tsx, which currently read as a version of the competitor's grid.
Work 4

Making the seven modules iconic

You cannot make seven modules excellent at once. A bar that applies to all of them, one signature moment each, and an order.

The bar — six tests, per module

  • 1Ten seconds. Cold start to a logged item, one hand, no typing where a tap will do.
  • 2One signature moment. The thing that gets screenshotted.
  • 3Empty states teach. Never a blank screen with an icon — show what it looks like in use.
  • 4Motion where it means something. The tick landing, the rung unlocking. Nowhere else.
  • 5It survives the plane. Fully usable offline — you have this architecturally and are not claiming it.
  • 6It leaves the app. A widget entry, a share card, or a notification action.

Two things stand out from that table. Journal and Notes have the highest ratio of impact to work — the backlink panel is a view over data that already exists, and “On this day” is a query plus a card. And Goals, Study and Sleep are mostly surfacing work that was already done and then buried.

The order

  • 1Habits — the braid ships with the rebrand; it is the signature image.
  • 2Journal — “On this day” is why people return daily rather than dutifully.
  • 3Notes — backlinks, the cheapest real differentiator on the list.
  • 4Tasks — quick capture; the most-used module, so the ten-second test bites hardest here.
  • 5Goals, Study, Sleep — surface what exists; smallest gaps.
